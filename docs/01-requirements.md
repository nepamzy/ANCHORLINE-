(committed to this repo for reference — do not edit).

## Business

- Anchorline Project Partners: independent construction oversight, quantity
  surveying, and project management consultancy, Abuja-based.
- Principal Consultant: Damian Chibueze Agu.
- Primary audience: Nigerians in the diaspora building/renovating/investing
  in property they cannot personally supervise.
- Positioning pillars (must carry through the whole site): technically
  qualified, independent, transparent, diaspora-friendly, structured
  engagement, nationwide reach (Abuja-based + arranged coverage).
- Objectives: establish credibility fast, educate on the 3-tier model,
  convert to WhatsApp/form enquiries, make the enquiry→engagement pipeline
  transparent, be maintainable by the client without a developer.

## Service model (locked — do not rename/add/remove)

| Tier | For | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| WATCH | Self-build clients wanting reassurance | Monthly site visit, dated photo/video documentation, brief written progress report |
| VERIFY | Clients making staged contractor payments | Everything in Watch + Bill of Quantities review + milestone certification |
| MANAGE | Developers & larger private projects | Full project/construction management — contractor engagement support, contract administration, cost control, quality supervision |

No public fixed pricing — Services page stays quote-driven.

## Page-by-page requirements

- **Home**: hero (tagline "Your eyes on the ground, wherever you are.",
  one-line description, "Get a Quote" CTA + WhatsApp CTA), trust strip
  ("Independent · Transparent · Technically Qualified"), 3-tier snapshot,
  How It Works teaser (3–4 steps), closing CTA banner.
- **About**: Principal Consultant bio/credentials (Section 3 of brief),
  narrative on why independent oversight matters, approach (honest
  reporting, no contractor relationship, written engagements).
- **Services**: each tier gets its own card/section (not a table row).
  Quote-driven, no fixed fees.
- **How It Works**: exact 5 steps — Enquiry, Scoping call, Engagement
  letter, First visit & report, Ongoing cadence.
- **Sample Report**: preview only (image previews or embedded/downloadable
  PDF — not raw editable content). Structure: Summary of Visit,
  Photographic/Video Documentation, Progress Assessment table (Foundation,
  Superstructure, Roofing, Electrical/Plumbing, Finishing, Site Safety —
  On Track / Minor Delay / Attention Required), Cost/Milestone Notes
  (Verify+), Recommendations & Next Steps, Next Scheduled Visit.
- **Coverage Area**: Abuja-based, standard cadence for FCT at no extra
  travel cost; adjusted cadence + optional remote video walkthrough for
  interstate; travel/logistics agreed individually.
- **FAQ**: 5 questions specified in brief (trust/independence, outside
  Abuja, developer-purchased units, privacy, how to start). Plain-language
  only — never publish internal scope exclusions or fee mechanics verbatim.
- **Testimonials**: launch with placeholder or omit — no client feedback
  supplied yet.
- **Contact / Get a Quote**: fields — name, email/phone, project location,
  project stage, tier of interest (dropdown), message. Submits to
  alprojectpartners@gmail.com. WhatsApp (0806 757 0941) equally weighted.

## Functional / technical — must-have

- Fully mobile-responsive (most diaspora visitors on phones).
- Fast-loading, clean, minimal — no heavy animation or clutter.
- WhatsApp click-to-chat button on every page.
- Contact/quote form emailing directly to the business, with spam
  protection.
- Client-editable text/images post-launch without a developer.
- HTTPS/SSL, reasonably fast hosting.

## Functional / technical — nice-to-have (not required for launch)

- Short explainer video/animated graphic on the reporting process.
- Expandable testimonials section as feedback is collected.
- Simple blog/Insights section (Phase 2 per brief).
- Basic analytics (visits, form conversions).
